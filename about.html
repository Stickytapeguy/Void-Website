Our Manifesto

Why we built Void.

1 / Philosophy

Zero Telemetry

Your hardware belongs to you. We don't track your usage, force software updates at 3 AM, or harvest your data to serve tailored ads on your desktop.

2 / Independence

No Cloud Hostage

You shouldn't have to log into an online server just to use the software you already paid for locally. Local accounts are standard, always.

3 / Ecosystem

Open Architecture

Modular software, predictable codebases, and independent distribution channels that bypass corporate app store gatekeepers and ridiculous fees.

Origins

How Void started.

It started out of sheer frustration. Every time you boot up a modern machine, you're forced to fight background telemetry, bypass forced cloud login prompts, and clean out software you never asked for. Hardware you bought and paid for starts acting like a rental device.

We decided to build the alternative. Void isn't backed by venture capital or corporate shareholders looking to monetize every click. It's a return to local independence, clean codebases, and tools that actually respect the user.
